# Period-induced translation symmetry classification

For the fixed 16-prime data in code/verify/pr92_walsh_distribution.py, let n=72k, k positive, and L_act be the intersection of active local period lattices. Define K=(L_act+4Z²)/4Z². Then K=C4 x C2 (even vertical coordinate) if gcd(k,5873)=1, and K=C4 x C4 otherwise. This is a classification of period-induced translations, not a claim about all XOR stabilizers or transposition lifts.

Proof: for each p, d=ord(P), e=d/gcd(n,d) divides D=d/gcd(72,d). For each divisor e of D, put g=d/e and R_e={z/g mod e: z in qualifying residues, g divides z}. Active support for unit u=n/g mod e is H_e intersect u^{-1}R_e, H_e=<2,3> mod e. Exhaustive finite check of every divisor and unit gives the following complete list of even-order quotients and the additional cases at 46933:
p=12907: e=5, |H|=4, R empty; e=2155, |H|=172, R={270,1885}, zero hits for all 1720 units (both targets nonunits).
p=46933: e=1, |H|=1, R empty; e=7, |H|=6, R empty; e=839, |H|=419, R={48,791}, exactly one hit for all 838 units; e=5873, |H|=2514, R={336,718,5155,5537}, exactly one hit for all 5028 units.
All other quotients have odd order. At e=5873 the lattice is generated by (1257,0),(-37,2), so its image mod4 consists exactly of even vertical coordinates. Intersecting with odd-index lattices does not change that image: multiply any vector in this lattice by an odd common multiple of the other indices congruent to 1 mod4. When no even active quotient exists the intersection has odd index and surjects mod4. Since e=5873/gcd(k,5873), this proves the stated classification.

Exact densities 5028/5873 and 845/5873. The 1000-seed scan yields 857 and 143; the extra full-symmetry seed beyond multiples of 7 is k=839.
