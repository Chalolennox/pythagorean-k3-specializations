## Proof

Let Tinf=Q(J[2^infty]), Linf=L0 Tinf, N=L0(1/2 M). By the first-level note, N/L0 has degree 8 and its defining square-class space V has three independent generators B1,B2,B3 indexed by opposite root pairs. Conjugation of i replaces B by its equal class. The Galois action on pairs contains a 3-cycle. Choose an element of 3-power order inducing it (take the odd-primary part of a lift of a 3-cycle). Since J has rational 2-torsion, Gal(Tinf/Q) is pro-2. This element fixes L0 intersect Tinf and extends to L0 Tinf fixing Tinf pointwise. Its conjugation action on Gal(L0 Tinf/L0) is trivial, by restriction to Tinf. Hence every class of V becoming square in Linf is fixed by the 3-cycle, so lies in the line generated by beta=B1 B2 B3. This uses the natural equivariance of quadratic Kummer characters.

The standard full 4-division field formula for roots 0,1+i,1-i gives L0(J[4])=L0(sqrt(1+i)): sqrt(2i)=1+i and the product (1+i)(1-i)=2 is already square. The owner certificate above identifies this with L0(sqrt(beta)), a nontrivial quadratic extension by independence of the three B classes. Thus exactly the invariant line disappears.

Consequently N intersect Linf=L0(J[4]), [N Linf:Linf]=4, and the translation image modulo 2 has dimension exactly 2. Its index in Z_2^14 is divisible by 4096; equality is not proved. No higher-level image or weighted correction factor or Problem H conclusion is claimed.
