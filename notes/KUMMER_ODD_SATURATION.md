Let J: y²=x³−2x²+2x, K the splitting field of the twelve points above H(1−x)=0, M generated by P and six opposite-pair representatives U_j. Use the previously attested signed Galois group and independence theorem.

At p=2621 the divisor splits completely with distinct nonzero y coordinates over F_p in the sense that all six distinct x roots have two nonzero y lifts. Good reduction and #J(F_p)=2680 exclude 3- and 23-primary torsion over K by prime-to-p torsion injectivity. At each witness prime the same splitting test holds; the primes avoid the discriminant and resultant bad primes 2,3,23.

For l=3, p=113819, N=113730, [N/3]P=(101754,42501). At p=49253, N=49536, U=(3857,14240), [N/3]U=(26339,2137). For l=23, p=229469, N=229080, [N/23]P=(202137,196012), U=(5468,173790), [N/23]U=(215498,199718). All nonzero. If a point were l-divisible modulo global torsion, its reduction would be l-divisible: global torsion has order prime to l and multiplication by l is invertible on it. Multiplication by N/l would then annihilate the reduction, contradiction. Transitivity transfers the U witness to every geometric representative.

The kernel of M/lM -> J(K)/(lJ(K)+torsion) is G-stable. For odd l each individual sign flip s_i isolates the i-th coordinate via (1-s_i)/2. A kernel vector with any nonzero U coordinate would therefore imply a U basis vector in the kernel, impossible by the witness. The remaining kernel lies on the P line, also excluded. Thus the map is injective at both 3 and 23, and the saturation index in the torsion-free Mordell–Weil group is coprime to 69.

Scope: saturation over K only. Saturation over K(J[l^infty]), actual Kummer image, cross-prime entanglement, local correction factors and C remain open. A central scalar -I in the torsion image over K would give H^1(G,J[l])=0 by Sah's lemma, but that scalar condition has not been established. No claim on Problem H.
