## Full rational self-map monoid

Let C be the auxiliary elliptic curve, with rational group Z P + (Z/2) T. Every nonconstant rational self-map over Q extends to a morphism and, by elliptic rigidity, is Q -> [m]Q+U, m a nonzero integer, U rational. Indeed the derivative of a rational endomorphism at the origin lies in Q; in characteristic zero this embeds the endomorphism ring into Q, and endomorphisms are integral, so End_Q(C)=Z.

Composition is (m,U)(n,V)=(mn,U+mV). For any homomorphism rho of this monoid into a finite group G, let g=rho(translation by U), h=rho([N]), N=|G|. The identity [N] tau_U=tau_U^N [N] implies h g h^-1=g^N=1. All translations, including T, are killed. The image is abelian, generated by commuting multiplication maps. Conversely every finite abelian group is an abstract quotient: assign generators to distinct primes and map m to the product of generators raised to their valuations in |m|. This is not an action on parity labels.

If a finite-valued configuration function on all C(Q) is equivariant under every nonconstant rational self-map with induced permutations, translations act trivially and transitively on C(Q), hence the function is constant. Assign a value at O if needed; the theorem assumes a total equivariant function. This excludes any nonconstant finite permutation factor, not merely a regular group of order 32. It does not exclude selected maps, noninvertible actions, or piecewise operations. No prime cutoff or parity distribution assumption enters.

For a selected multiplier set S, the order of an induced translation permutation must be coprime to every m in S. This follows from conjugacy to its m-th power.

## Rank and correspondences

Two rational points on C cannot be Mordell-Weil independent: if Q1=nP+epsilon T and Q2=mP+delta T, then 2mQ1-2nQ2=O (if both indices zero, both are torsion). C x C does have rank two as a product, but is a different state space and was rejected as outside the original objective.

By Mordell-Lang, a geometrically irreducible algebraic curve in C x C with infinitely many rational pairs is a translate of an elliptic subgroup. For both projections nonconstant it is described by [a]Q1+[b]Q2=U with coprime nonzero integers a,b and U rational. To see the subgroup description, an elliptic subgroup has an isogeny from C by a dual of a nonzero projection; its two coordinates are rational endomorphisms of C (the subgroup is defined over Q by density of rational points). Divide the resulting integer coefficients by their gcd to obtain the primitive kernel, which is connected by Bezout. A rational point of the translate gives rational U. For non-geometrically irreducible curves, apply the argument to components with infinitely many rational points; finite exceptional pairs are not classified by this statement.

On the rank-one orbit this gives an+bm=k and a epsilon+b delta=eta mod 2. Each single index branch is affine rational, n -> rn+s. A finite-order such map on an infinite invariant index set has order at most two, since r is a rational root of unity and r=1 forces s=0. This excludes exact order four on the free index, not necessarily on a torsion-enriched state or on a finite quotient of labels. F.57(e) already supplies the translation-gap correspondences. Correspondences on the parameter line can be pulled back to C x C; the classification concerns components supporting infinitely many lifted rational pairs.

## Piecewise construction and unresolved parity compatibility

Write n=32k+16e+4j+i, i,j in {0,1,2,3}, e in {0,1}. Define X increment i mod 4, Y increment j mod 4, S swap i,j and toggle e. These act regularly on each 32-index block, with X^4=Y^4=S^2=1, XY=YX, SXS=Y. Exact JavaScript checks on indices -64 through 95 and orbit enumeration of 0 returned all relations true and orbit size 32. Symbolically X is translation by P or -3P; Y by 4P or -12P; S by (16(1-2e)+3(i-j))P. Branch selection is congruence-dependent, not a single algebraic morphism or a uniquely determined algebraic correspondence without the selection rule.

This is an abstract index action, not a demonstrated action on the complete parity blocks B_n=(a(2^i3^j n)) for 0<=i,j<4. If it intertwines the regular reference action on a positive block containing a valid array, all 32 arrays must occur exactly once there. Consequently for every grid position (i,j), sum over r=0,...,31 of a(2^i3^j(32k+r)) must equal 16. These necessary balance identities are not sufficient and are not established. No complete parity factorization or numerical balance test was executed. Infinitely many such positive blocks would imply Problem H, but the construction gives no proof of their existence. No general impossibility theorem for piecewise correspondences is claimed.

## Closure

Close this exploratory direction: full rational self-map permutation descent is excluded; irreducible correspondences are structurally restricted; piecewise correspondences realize the abstract group but do not control parity. The original parity distribution obstacle remains. This note records algebraic deductions and the finite index-action verification, not a proof of Problem H or of impossibility of all nonlinear transformations.
